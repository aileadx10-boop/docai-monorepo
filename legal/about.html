Our Story

Built on Real Experience

BizLegal AI was born from a simple observation: businesses were paying $5,000-$15,000 for routine commercial legal documents that should cost a fraction of that amount. Meanwhile, solo entrepreneurs and small businesses were using free templates that exposed them to massive legal risks.

Our founder, a New York-licensed attorney with 20+ years of international commercial law practice, decided to bridge this gap. The solution: combine deep legal expertise with advanced AI to make institutional-quality legal intelligence accessible to everyone.

Every template, every analysis, every recommendation in our platform is built on the foundation of hundreds of millions of dollars in actual transaction experience. This isn't AI trained on internet text — this is AI powered by real deal-making expertise.
