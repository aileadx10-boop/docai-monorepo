How to request

Email [email protected] within 24 hours with your order ID and reason. Refunds processed within 3–5 business days to your original payment method.

Due Diligence Reports are non-refundable once the analysis has been delivered to your email. The AI analysis is performed immediately upon payment confirmation and the results are sent instantly.

If a generated document is clearly defective (e.g., empty, corrupted, or missing all content), we will either re-generate at no charge or provide a full refund. This does not apply to documents you consider incomplete because AI outputs do not meet your subjective expectations — AI drafting is a starting point, not a final product.

We take payment disputes seriously. Filing a chargeback for a delivered digital product without first contacting us is considered fraud and may result in legal action. Please contact us first — we resolve legitimate disputes promptly.
