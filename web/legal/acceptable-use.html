Effective: April 2026 · DocAI by BizLegal AI

This policy governs how you may use DocAI, the document generation and due diligence platform operated by BizLegal AI. By using DocAI, you agree to comply with this policy.

1. Permitted Use

You may use DocAI to:

  • Generate legal document templates for your own transactions
  • Upload contracts for AI-powered due diligence review
  • Use generated documents as starting points for further legal review
  • Share reports and documents internally within your organisation
  • Use DocAI as a professional tool if you are a lawyer, broker, or advisor

2. Prohibited Use

You may NOT use DocAI to:

  • Resell, redistribute, or white-label generated documents or reports without written permission

All documents and reports generated by DocAI are created using artificial intelligence. While the template architecture is informed by legal expertise, outputs may contain errors or omissions. You are responsible for reviewing all outputs with a licensed attorney before execution.

You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of all information and files you submit to DocAI. Submitted contracts for due diligence are processed by the AI and are not stored after report delivery. Do not submit documents containing classified, restricted, or unlawfully obtained information.

DocAI enforces rate limits to ensure fair access for all users. Excessive requests, automated scraping, or abuse of API endpoints may result in temporary or permanent suspension.
