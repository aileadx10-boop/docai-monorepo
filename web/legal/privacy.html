Privacy Policy

Effective: April 2026 · Last updated: April 2026

BizLegal AI ("we", "us") is operated by BizLegal AI. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads.

1. Data We Collect

When you purchase a document

  • Email address (for document delivery)
  • Deal details you enter in the form (party names, amounts, dates)
  • Payment data — processed by PayPal. We never see or store card numbers.

When you upload for Due Diligence

  • Email address
  • The contract file you upload (PDF or DOCX)
  • The contract is passed to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis and is not stored by us after delivery

Automatically collected

  • Browser type, IP address, pages visited — via Vercel analytics (anonymized)
  • We do not use Google Analytics or third-party ad trackers

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate and deliver your document or report
  • To send your document by email (SendGrid)
  • To process your payment (PayPal)
  • To respond to support requests
  • We do not use your data for marketing without explicit consent

3. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI document generation and Due Diligence analysis. Data processed per Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • PayPal — Payment processing. Subject to PayPal's privacy policy.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — Email delivery of documents.
  • Vercel — Hosting and serverless functions.
  • Supabase — Lead management database (outreach program only — not linked to purchases).

4. Data Retention

  • Purchase email addresses: retained for 2 years for support purposes
  • Uploaded contracts (DD): deleted after analysis delivery, not stored
  • Payment records: retained as required by applicable tax law
  • You may request deletion at any time: [email protected]

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Data portability
  • California residents: right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)

To exercise any right: [email protected]

6. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for payment processing (PayPal) and session management. No advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Children

BizLegal AI is not directed at persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
