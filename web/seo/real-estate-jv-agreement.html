Essential JV Agreement Clauses

A comprehensive real estate JV agreement in UAE must include:

  • Capital Contributions: Clear specification of cash, property, or services contributed by each party
  • Profit Distribution: Detailed waterfall structure for returns, preferred returns, and promote structures
  • Decision-Making: Voting rights, major decision thresholds, and dead-lock resolution mechanisms
  • Exit Strategy: Buy-sell provisions, drag-along/tag-along rights, and transfer restrictions
  • Non-Circumvention: Protection against parties bypassing the JV to deal directly
  • Dispute Resolution: Arbitration clauses specifying DIFC-LCIA or ADGM arbitration

UAE-Specific Considerations

Real estate JVs in Dubai face unique legal requirements:

  • Ownership Restrictions: Foreign ownership limits in certain areas (freehold vs leasehold)
  • Local Partner Requirements: Some structures require UAE national participation
  • RERA Compliance: Dubai Land Department regulations for property investments
  • Tax Considerations: VAT implications and potential corporate tax obligations

Common JV Mistakes to Avoid

  • Vague profit-sharing formulas leading to disputes
  • Missing non-circumvention protections
  • Inadequate exit mechanisms
  • Poorly defined decision-making processes
  • Missing insurance and liability provisions
