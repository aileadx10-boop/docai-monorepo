VARA Compliance Checklist 2025: Complete Guide for Crypto Businesses

📅 Updated January 2025 ⏱️ 12 min read 👤 By Advocate Dor, NY Bar

Operating a crypto business in Dubai requires strict compliance with the Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA). This comprehensive checklist, built on 20+ years of commercial law expertise, covers everything you need to know about VARA licensing, ongoing compliance, and regulatory requirements.

What is VARA?

The Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) is Dubai's dedicated regulator for virtual assets and crypto businesses. Established in 2022, VARA oversees all virtual asset activities across Dubai (excluding DIFC, which has its own framework).

Key Point: Any business conducting virtual asset activities in Dubai must obtain VARA authorization. Operating without proper licensing can result in significant fines, business closure, and potential criminal liability.

📋 VARA Licensing Checklist

1. Determine Your Activity Type

VARA regulates specific activities: Exchange Services, Broker-Dealer Services, Custody Services, Transfer Services, Payment Services, and Advisory Services. Identify which activities your business conducts.

2. Business Structure Requirements

Your entity must be properly structured in Dubai (mainland or free zone). VARA requires clear ownership structure, fit-and-proper tests for shareholders, and adequate capital requirements.

3. Compliance Framework

Implement comprehensive AML/CFT policies, KYC procedures, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring systems. VARA requires appointment of a Compliance Officer with relevant experience.

4. Technology & Security

Demonstrate robust cybersecurity measures, secure custody solutions, business continuity planning, and disaster recovery capabilities. Third-party audits may be required.

5. Financial Requirements

Meet minimum capital requirements based on your activity type. Maintain adequate professional indemnity insurance. Prepare audited financial statements.

VARA Licensing Process

The VARA licensing process typically takes 8-16 weeks and involves multiple stages:

  1. Initial Application: Submit preliminary documentation and pay application fees
  2. Review Period: VARA reviews your application and may request additional information
  3. In-Principle Approval: Receive conditional approval to proceed
  4. Final Inspection: VARA conducts final review of systems and procedures
  5. License Issuance: Receive full operating license

Ongoing Compliance Requirements

Once licensed, you must maintain ongoing compliance with VARA regulations:

  • Annual Renewals: Submit renewal applications and pay annual fees
  • Financial Reporting: Submit audited financial statements annually
  • Compliance Reporting: Regular AML/CFT reports and suspicious activity reports
  • Material Changes: Notify VARA of any material changes to business, ownership, or operations
  • Record Keeping: Maintain all required records for minimum 5 years

Common VARA Compliance Mistakes

Based on our experience advising crypto businesses, here are the most common compliance failures:

  • Inadequate KYC procedures — Not verifying customer identities properly
  • Weak AML controls — Insufficient transaction monitoring and sanctions screening
  • Poor record keeping — Failing to maintain required documentation
  • Unauthorized activities — Conducting activities beyond licensed scope
  • Delayed reporting — Missing regulatory filing deadlines

VARA vs DIFC: Which Framework Applies?

Dubai has two regulatory frameworks for crypto businesses:

  • VARA: Covers Dubai mainland and most free zones
  • DIFC (DFSA): Covers Dubai International Financial Centre only

Your choice depends on where you want to establish your business. DIFC offers a more established common law framework but higher costs. VARA provides dedicated crypto regulation with potentially faster licensing.

Penalties for Non-Compliance

VARA has significant enforcement powers. Penalties for non-compliance include:

  • Financial Penalties: Fines up to AED 10 million for serious violations
  • License Suspension: Temporary or permanent suspension of operating license
  • Criminal Liability: Potential criminal charges for severe violations
  • Reputational Damage: Public enforcement actions can damage business reputation
